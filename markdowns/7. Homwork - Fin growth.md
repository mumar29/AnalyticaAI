# Homework: Financial Growth Over Time (2004–2024)

Imagine it's 2004, and you saved 10,000 PKR to invest. Your goal is to maximize your returns by the year 2024.

# 1. Investment Options:

- You may choose one or more from the following asset classes:
- - Gold: Historical prices from 2004 to 2024.
- Silver: Historical prices from 2004 to 2024.
- Foreign Currency: Buy and hold USD or other currencies using historical PKR-USD rates.
- Stock Market:
- Invest in individual stocks (e.g., Lucky Cement, Engro foods, OGDC etc).
- Invest in the KSE-100 index.
- Mutual Funds: Open-end and close-end funds (e.g, Al Meezan mutual fund).
- Savings Accounts: Fixed deposits or regular saving accounts with annual interest rates.
- Any other similar asset available in Pakistan (sarmaaya.pk, tradingeconomics.com)

You cannot invest in global assets (e.g., S&P500, VTSAX, cryptocurrencies).

# 2. Zakat Obligation:

- You must pay 2.5% zakat annually (31 Dec) on the total value of your assets.
- Zakat can be paid by liquidating part of an asset or from cash holdings.

# 3. Capital Gains Tax (CGT):

- A 15% capital gains tax applies when selling an asset.
- CGT is calculated on the difference between the selling price and the purchase price.
- You must hold each bought asset for at least 11 months.

# 4. Miscellaneous:

- Each transaction (buy or sell) incurs a 1% transaction fee of the transaction amount.
- You must maintain at least 10% of your net worth in cash.
