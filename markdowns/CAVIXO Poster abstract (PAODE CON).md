# CAVIXO: Redefining Dental Care Accessibility through Voice-Controlled and Inclusive Mobile Technology

Miraal Umar, Rayan Mansoor, Shafa-at Ali Sheikh

National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST)

October 24, 2024

## Abstract

As the global population ages and the demand for accessible healthcare services grows, it becomes imperative to develop solutions that cater to the unique needs of elderly and differently abled patients. Traditional dental appointment systems often fall short in addressing the mobility, digital literacy, and visual impairments prevalent in these demographics. CAVIXO is an innovative mobile application designed specifically to overcome these barriers and revolutionize the way dental services are accessed.

Harnessing the power of voice-controlled technology and a user-centric design approach, CAVIXO allows patients to navigate the app using simple, natural language commands in both English and Urdu. The app incorporates advanced accessibility features, including customizable font sizes, drag-and-drop UI elements, and colorblind modes, ensuring ease of use for individuals with visual impairments such as Protanopia and Deuteranopia. By streamlining essential functionalities like appointment scheduling, reminders, treatment history, and billing, CAVIXO empowers patients to independently manage their dental care with minimal effort.

Designed with healthcare professionals in mind, CAVIXO enhances patient satisfaction while optimizing the administrative workflow in dental practices. The app's multilingual voice commands eliminate complex menu navigation, making it a valuable tool for practices serving diverse populations or patients with limited
technological proficiency. This inclusive design expands access to dental care and paves the way for future integration with telemedicine and AI-powered health tools.

In summary, CAVIXO represents a significant leap forward in the accessibility of dental services, offering a seamless, inclusive, and technologically advanced solution to bridge the gap between patients and dental care.
